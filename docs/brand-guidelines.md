# K&H Online Store — Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity & Vision
- **Brand Name**: K&H Online Store
- **Arabic Slogan**: كل ما يهم المرأة و المنزل (*Everything that matters to woman & home*)
- **Positioning**: A premier curated boutique destination offering exquisite selections across women's fashion, handbags, beauty, perfumes, kitchenware, and modern home decor for discerning clients in Tunisia and beyond.
- **Brand Essence**: Elegance, Warmth, Quality, and Comfort.

---

## 2. Official Color Palette
The brand color scheme reflects feminine luxury, tactile warmth, and timeless editorial refinement:

---

## 3. Typography
- **Arabic Headings**: Amiri (`font-serif`) — Classic Arabic calligraphy elegance.
- **Arabic Body & Navigation**: Cairo (`font-sans`) — Clear, legible, modern geometric Arabic sans-serif.
- **Latin Headings**: Cormorant Garamond (`font-serif`) — High-fashion editorial serif with graceful contrast.
- **Latin Body**: Plus Jakarta Sans (`font-sans`) — Modern clean proportions with supreme readability.

---

## 4. Logo Usage
The K&H insignia consists of:
- Interlocking monogram **K&H** in refined serif styling.
- Subtitle: **ONLINE STORE**.
- Arabic Tagline: **كل ما يهم المرأة و المنزل**.
- Minimum clear space is maintained around the insignia at all times.
- Displayed prominently in header navigation, footer, invoice printouts, auth screens, and administrative dashboard.

---

## 5. Tone of Voice & Editorial Guidelines
- **Warm & Welcoming**: Greeting visitors with respectful warmth (*أهلاً بكِ في عالم K&H*).
- **Curated & Discerning**: Highlighting product craftsmanship, fabric details, notes of fragrance, and interior harmony.
- **Uncompromising Trust**: Transparent pricing in Tunisian Dinars (TND), cash on delivery assurance, and dedicated customer care.
